    An episode starts at an alert hour that follows a non-alert hour. The formal evaluation counts alert hours, not episodes. In about a third of shifts nobody develops sepsis; that is how a ward really looks.

Stays usually end a few hours after onset, so the right edge thins out.

    Research demonstration. Not for clinical use.

    Method: the challenge label turns on 6 hours before clinical onset, so onset is the first positive hour plus 6. The model's calibration and the alert threshold were fit on hospital A only. Hospital B was used only to evaluate. Every number on this page reproduces the evaluation file the model was scored with.
